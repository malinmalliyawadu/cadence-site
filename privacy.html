Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Cadence keeps your training on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your plans, logged sets, settings and imported workouts are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple Health

With your permission, Cadence reads runs, yoga and strength workouts, workout effort, heart rate, active energy and bodyweight from Apple Health to measure training load and set run paces and heart-rate zones, and reads heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep to judge how recovered you are. It writes the strength sessions you finish back to Apple Health as workouts with their effort and active energy. On Apple Watch, Cadence records a live strength workout with your heart rate and energy and mirrors it to your iPhone. Health data is read and written on your device. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never sold.

Location and weather

To fetch the forecast, Cadence sends the coordinates of your chosen place to Apple Weather. If you tap Use my location, that place is your device's current location, which Cadence asks for only while the app is in use. The request goes from your device to Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer does not receive it.

Imports

A CSV file you import is read on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

Children

Cadence is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
