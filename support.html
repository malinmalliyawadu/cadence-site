Support

Something not working, or a question about how Cadence plans your week? Open an issue and you will get a reply.

Runs and yoga are not logging

Open Settings in Cadence, then Apple Health, and connect. Cadence reads workouts recorded by Apple Watch or the Fitness app. If you connected before, check the iPhone Settings app, Health, Data Access and Devices, Cadence, and make sure reading workouts is on.

The forecast is for the wrong place

Open Settings in Cadence, then Forecast, and tap Use my location. Until you do, Cadence uses a default city.

A weight looks wrong

Cadence sets each weight from the reps in reserve you give on the final set of a movement. Rate that set honestly and the next session adjusts. You can change any planned set's weight or reps before you lift.

Bringing in old workouts

Cadence can import a CSV workout export from Settings, Import. Days that already hold a logged session are left alone.

Starting over

Settings, Delete all sessions removes every session and plan. Your settings stay.
